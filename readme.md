# Dual-Licensing Template

For absolute freedom of my project, I require a license aligned with the philosophy: "**NO RIGHTS RESERVED; USE AT YOUR OWN RISK, NO WARRANTY**." This dual-licensing template, utilizing the Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal (CC0 1.0) and Zero-Clause BSD (0BSD) licenses, effectively meets this requirement. While I intend to license templates, documentations, and specifications under CC0 1.0 and scripts, programs, and databases under 0BSD, users retain the freedom to choose either license, regarding their preferance or juridical circumstance.
